Our Offerings

We offer a wide range of services to meet your needs:

Creative Expression. Inner Healing. Mindful Living.
We offer a unique blend of traditional artistry, therapeutic practices and meditative techniques, carefully curated to support emotional well-being and inner peace. All services are available online or in-person based on individual, group or organizational needs

Teaching Online classes for Traditional Art of Tibet & India

Learn sacred and cultural art forms from India and Tibet such as Basic Thangka, Pichwai, Saura & advanced Mandala art in structured online classes. Each traditional style offers not just technique but a meditative journey through culture and inner stillness.

Creative Art Therapy Based Healing workshop

A gentle, expressive approach to emotional wellness through art. These sessions offer a safe space to explore feelings, release inner blocks, and support healing using color, form and creativity as therapeutic tools.Ideal for stress, anxiety, emotional overwhelm or simply reconnecting with yourself.

Mandala & Meditative Art workshops

Experience the calming power of mandalas sacred circular patterns that promote mindfulness, focus and emotional balance. These sessions blend intuitive art making with meditative stillness, helping you reconnect with your inner self.No artistic experience needed just an open heart and a willingness to explore.

Breathwork & Mindfulness Practices

Learn a variety of breathing techniques like deep belly breathing, alternate nostril (Nadi Shodhana) and box breathing to calm the nervous system and center the mind.Our meditation sessions include mindfulness, Vipassana,body scan & visualization designed to cultivate presence, emotional clarity and inner peace.Each session is gently guided and accessible to all levels
