The Story Behind the Art, Education and Healing

At Utkalaa Arts, we blend the power of traditional art with mindful expression—helping you find peace, presence, and emotional renewal through creativity. Founded by Bismita Aruk, a passionate & professional traditional artist, art educator, art therapy practitioner, and meditation guide. Utkalaa Arts was born from years of personal exploration, cultural reverence, and the desire to help others connect with their inner selves through art.

Though there is no physical studio space yet, Utkalaa Arts comes alive wherever it is needed through online and offline workshops, community collaborations and one-on-one sessions with individuals, groups and organizations.

Meet the Artist & Founder – Bismita Aruk

“In my creation, I Meditate. Every dot, line and color is a prayer in motion.”

Bismita Aruk is an accomplished Indian artist creating art from past 7 years. Her work reflects in her intricate designs. She specializes in Mandala Art & different traditional art forms like Thangka, Pichwai & Saura paintings. She learned mastery of these traditional art styles from her mentors who have preserved and passed down their craft through generations.

Utkalaa Arts is born from a deep-rooted desire to blend creativity with connection-to ourselves, our community, and our emotional well-being. Her mission is to help individuals to overcome mental and emotional traumas through creative exporation and the goal is to reach out to as many people as possible to offer a journy of self-discovery and healing through art.

As an Art Educator

Teaching art is one of her greatest joys. She conducts art classes and workshops for children, adults, and educators. Each session is crafted not just to teach techniques, but to inspire creative confidence and self-expression. From beginners to seasoned artists, she welcomes all who seek to explore their creative side.

Her Art Therapy Practice

As an art therapy practitioner, she works with individuals and groups to support mental, emotional and spiritual well-being through art & meditation. She believe in the healing power of creativity and offer safe, non-judgmental spaces for exploration, growth and healing. Whether you're facing stress, trauma or simply need a reflective outlet, these sessions are tailored to meet you where you are.


Let's Conect

Whether you're looking to explore your artistic side, learn new skills or embark on a healing journey, Utkala Arts welcomes you. Feel free to reach out for collaborations & sessions.
